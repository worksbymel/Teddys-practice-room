Study references & content notes 📚

The question bank is original practice content. It is informed by the official JLPT N3 test-item blueprint and reputable Japanese-learning grammar references; it is not copied from official past papers and is not endorsed by JLPT.

  • JLPT: Test sections and item types

Quality note: these are original study items, not JLPT-certified materials. The vocabulary meaning checks use English glosses, and the reading set is short-form practice rather than a complete simulation of every N3 reading type. The full bank has not been individually reviewed by a native-language assessment editor. Treat explanations as study guidance, and verify uncertain points with a teacher or trusted dictionary.
